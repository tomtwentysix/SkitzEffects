Skitz Effects

Audio effects for everything you play and record, with 5.1 and 7.1 sound on any device.

Install on Fedora Source on GitHub

What it does

Presets you already have

Compatible with Easy Effects presets, for playback and microphones.

Presets per device

Each output and input remembers its own preset and switches to it when you change device.

Surround on any device

5.1 and 7.1 input. Surround devices receive every channel, and stereo devices and headphones get a binaural downmix through a 14-channel HeSuVi HRIR.

Distortion Check

Play something typical, stop the check, and see which stages went over 0 dB. Skitz Effects can turn those stages down for you.

Noise removal

Deep Noise Remover cleans up your microphone with DeepFilterNet.

Crossfeed for headphones

Feeds a little of each channel into the other, as speakers reach both ears, so hard-panned stereo is easier on headphones.

Device names

Rename devices system-wide from the Devices page, so every app shows the names you chose.

How it works

Skitz Effects does no audio processing of its own. PipeWire's filter-chain hosts the LSP, ZAM, MDA and Calf LV2 plugins, its convolver handles impulse responses and the HRIR, and its biquad filters run Crossfeed. Skitz Effects builds the chain, keeps it in step with your devices and gives you a GTK 4 interface to edit it.

Install

Skitz Effects is packaged for Fedora in COPR. Updates then arrive with your other system updates.

sudo dnf copr enable solartw/skitz-effects
sudo dnf install skitz-effects

The package pulls in PipeWire's filter-chain LV2 host and the LSP, ZAM and MDA plugins. Bass Enhancer, Exciter, Stereo Tools, Reverberation and Deesser use the Calf plugins, and Deep Noise Remover uses DeepFilterNet's LADSPA plugin from the same COPR. Both are installed by default and can be removed.
